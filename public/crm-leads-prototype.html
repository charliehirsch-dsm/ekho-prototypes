Lead lifecycle & forwarding

How a Digital retail order lead moves from checkout to close: how its state changes and what updates in the Leads drawer. A lead is only created once Ekho has a name plus an email or phone; before that it's an anonymous checkout session with no lead record. Ekho forwards the lead to the dealer's own CRM/DMS exactly once (a one-time snapshot, no follow-up updates), at whichever terminal state the lead reaches. Only Abandoned is pre-placement; every other outcome happens after the order is placed (unit soft-locked). The snapshot always includes a deep link back to the live Ekho lead record, so the dealer can click through for current status even though Ekho never pushes updates.

Order status states: In progress during checkout. If the buyer ghosts before placing, the lead is Abandoned (the only pre-placement outcome). Otherwise they place an order (unit soft-locked, not yet a win), and it resolves to one of five post-placement states: Expired (soft-lock lapses), Cancelled (buyer cancels), Financing rejected, Declined financing offers, or Closed won (only on conversion). Each lead fires exactly one forward, at whichever terminal state it reaches. Because all five post-placement states have a real order, the drawer links out to it with Open in Orders.
